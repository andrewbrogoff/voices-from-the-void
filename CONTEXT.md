# Voices From The Void

A Minecraft magic mod in the lineage of Thaumcraft 4. The world is resonance, the Void is the silence beneath it, and the Voices are what a scholar hears when they listen to that silence.

## Language

### The Void

**Void**:
The silence beneath all resonance; what the world is struck upon. Not a place to visit by default, but what lies under every place.
_Avoid_: the Abyss, the Outside, the Far Lands

**Voices**:
What can be heard in the Void: the echoes of the Choir. Not malicious, no longer human, and they want to be heard.
_Avoid_: whispers (as a proper noun), Old Ones, the Eldritch

**Listening**:
The deliberate act of tuning in to the Void. It is always a choice the player makes, never something that happens to them.
_Avoid_: scrying, channelling

**Listener**:
One who has begun listening to the Void: the player's role in the fiction. Used sparingly.
_Avoid_: thaumaturge, mage, wizard

**Choir**:
The earlier order of scholars who listened too long and went into the Void; the Voices are what the Choir became. Its ruins and journal pages remain in the world.
_Avoid_: the Ancients, the Precursors

**Answerers**:
A living faction who speak back to the Voices without understanding them. Hostile, and found at Choir sites.
_Avoid_: cultists, the Cult

**Void-tongue**:
The invented language of the Voices, used only for eldritch-tier names and late Voice lines. It is breathy and liquid, with no hard stops.
_Avoid_: eldritch script, the Old Tongue

**Warp**:
How audible the Void finds you: your resonance with it, grown by listening and by Void-tier work. The share of it that is permanent is what can never be un-heard.
_Avoid_: sanity, corruption, madness

**Temporary warp**:
Warp that fades on its own within minutes.

**Lasting warp**:
Warp that stays until the Listener treats it. Treatment always works but always costs something.
_Avoid_: sticky warp, normal warp

**Permanent warp**:
The small share of warp that can never be removed, gained only from a few named milestone choices that show their exact cost beforehand.

**Gear warp**:
Warp that counts only while its item is carried, and goes when the item is put down.
_Avoid_: item warp

**Bargain**:
An offer from the Voices of power in exchange for more warp. It can always be refused at no cost.
_Avoid_: pact, deal

### Aspects and energy

**Aspect**:
One of the fundamental "notes" that every thing in the world resonates with. Aspects carry their Latin names (Aer, Ignis, Vacuos…).
_Avoid_: element, essence

**Primal**:
An aspect that is not made of others.
_Avoid_: base aspect, elemental

**Compound**:
An aspect made of exactly two lower aspects.
_Avoid_: complex aspect, derived aspect

**Vacuos**:
The aspect of the Void. It seems to be a compound, but listening reveals it to be a hidden primal that lies beneath the others.

**Unheard**:
An aspect the Listener has met but not yet discovered, because its parents aren't both known. Its amount still shows exactly; only its identity is withheld.
_Avoid_: unknown aspect, locked aspect

**Aspect points**:
A Listener's store of each aspect, earned by striking things for the first time and spent on research. Uncapped.
_Avoid_: research points, RP

**Vis**:
Free-flowing magical energy: resonance not bound to any object.
_Avoid_: mana, aura energy

**Essentia**:
An aspect distilled out of matter into a material, storable form.
_Avoid_: liquid aspect, aspect fluid

**Flux**:
The spilled residue of careless or unstable magic.
_Avoid_: pollution, miasma

**The Hush**:
Land or creatures where pooled flux has thinned the world until the Void seeps through. They are quiet, drained of colour, and hollowed.
_Avoid_: taint, corruption, blight

**Node**:
A naturally occurring knot of vis in the world, which can be found, tapped and cultivated.
_Avoid_: aura node, ley point

**Vis aspect**:
An aspect whose vis a wand can hold: each exposed primal. Six are built in (Aer, Terra, Ignis, Aqua, Ordo and Perditio), and an addon may add up to two more. Vacuos is not one.
_Avoid_: vis type, element

**Clarity**:
How cleanly a node rings: *faint*, *steady* or *clear*. A clearer node refills faster.
_Avoid_: brightness, purity

**Wellspring**:
The starter node: an anchored node holding the six built-in vis aspects, placed near spawn in every world so the first wand can be filled.
_Avoid_: spawn node, home node

**Tapping**:
Drawing vis from a node into a wand, by holding a wand to it or through a Node Tap. It never harms the node.
_Avoid_: draining, siphoning

**Cultivation**:
Feeding a node essentia through a Node Tap. A vis aspect's essentia raises the node's capacity for that aspect, and Sonus raises its clarity.
_Avoid_: node bullying, node feeding

### Craft

**Commonplace Book**:
The Listener's own research notebook, which serves as the in-game codex. Its prose grows less reliable as warp rises, but its recipes and numbers are always accurate. Choir pages found in ruins are tipped into it.
_Avoid_: codex (in player-facing text), Thaumonomicon, guidebook

**Note**:
A research puzzle in progress on a page of the Commonplace Book: a hex grid whose anchors the Listener joins with chains of linked aspects. It never uses ink and can never be unsolvable.
_Avoid_: research paper, theory

**Anchor**:
An aspect printed on a note before work begins. A note is solved when all its anchors are joined.
_Avoid_: key aspect, node (a node is a knot of vis)

**Choir page**:
A page from a Choir scholar's journal, found in ruins and tipped into the Commonplace Book. It is lucid where the Listener's own notes slip, and warp never degrades it.
_Avoid_: fragment, lost knowledge, lore page

**Tuning Fork**:
The first listening instrument. Striking it against a thing reveals that thing's aspects.
_Avoid_: scanner, Thaumometer

**Strike**:
Using the Tuning Fork on a block, entity, item or node to read its aspects. "Scanning" names the system in specs, never the act in player-facing text.
_Avoid_: scan (in player-facing text)

**Sounding**:
Striking the Tuning Fork at nothing to hear the bearing and distance of the nearest node.
_Avoid_: pinging, locating

**Resonant Brass**:
The first magical metal, made by attuning copper.
_Avoid_: Thaumium, magic brass

**Wand**:
The hand-held instrument that holds vis and spends it on arcane work.
_Avoid_: staff (unless it is a distinct tier), rod (which is a wand part)

**Rod**:
The shaft of a wand. It sets how much vis each of the wand's pools holds.
_Avoid_: core, staff

**Chime rod**:
The Stage 2 rod: a hollow tube of Resonant Brass.
_Avoid_: Greatwood, Silverwood

**Cap**:
One of the matching pair of fittings on a wand's ends. It sets how much the wand's work costs in vis.
_Avoid_: tip, ferrule

**Bell Jar**:
A glass dome that takes up a whole node, so it can be carried and set down elsewhere unchanged.
_Avoid_: node jar

**Node Tap**:
An ear-trumpet set against a node. A relay network draws vis through it, and essentia fed into it cultivates the node.
_Avoid_: node siphon, drain

**Vis Relay**:
A block that links Node Taps and Wand Cradles within its range into a relay network.
_Avoid_: pylon, transmitter

**Relay network**:
Node Taps, Vis Relays and Wand Cradles linked by range, carrying vis from nodes to wands. Player-facing text calls one a **chord**.
_Avoid_: grid, vis net

**Wand Cradle**:
A stand that holds one wand and charges it from its relay network.
_Avoid_: charger, pedestal

**Focus**:
An attachment that gives a wand a spell to cast. Plural: foci.
_Avoid_: spell, glyph

**Arcane crafting**:
Crafting that costs vis as well as materials.

**Worktable**:
The station for arcane crafting: a crafting table turned with a wand. The vis is paid from the wand set in it.
_Avoid_: Arcane Worktable, arcane workbench

**Crucible**:
A vessel of boiling water that dissolves thrown items into essentia and spends that essentia to transform a catalyst. Essentia never combines in it.
_Avoid_: cauldron (the vanilla block it is made from)

**Catalyst**:
The item thrown into a crucible that a recipe the thrower has learned transforms, paid for from the crucible's dissolved essentia.
_Avoid_: reagent, base item

**Essentia Jar**:
A glass jar that stores one aspect of essentia, keeps it when broken and carried, and shows that aspect's glyph on its face.
_Avoid_: warded jar, jar (alone, in player-facing text, where it could mean the Bell Jar)

**Label**:
A paper tag naming one aspect. On an Essentia Jar it locks the jar to that aspect; on an Essentia Tube it lets only that aspect pass.
_Avoid_: filter, tag

**Alembic**:
A still-head hung on a crucible's side that pours one aspect from the crucible into the vessel beneath its spout. It holds nothing itself.
_Avoid_: arcane alembic, distiller

**Essentia Tube**:
A brass-and-glass pipe that joins vessels into a line. It holds no essentia.
_Avoid_: pipe, conduit

**Valve**:
A tube fitting that closes a line by hand or under a redstone signal.
_Avoid_: tap (a Node Tap is something else), shutoff

**Tube network**:
Essentia Tubes and open Valves joined face to face, carrying essentia between the vessels they touch, one aspect into one vessel at a time. Player-facing text calls one a **line**, which is distinct from a vis **chord**.
_Avoid_: pipe network, grid

**Suction**:
How strongly a vessel pulls an aspect along a line right now. Essentia moves only toward a stronger suction than the rank it is held at.
_Avoid_: pressure, priority

**Hold**:
The rank at which a vessel keeps the essentia it already has. It does not change as the vessel fills, which is why a full jar never drains into an empty one beside it.
_Avoid_: retention, lock

**Infusion**:
Altar crafting that drives essentia into a central item.

**Golem**:
A crafted servant that carries out jobs it has been marked for.
_Avoid_: construct, automaton

**Eldritch**:
An adjective only, for things of the Void's deepest tier. Never a proper noun.

### Progression

**Stage**:
One of the four steps of the Listener's progression. A Stage is entered by finding something in the world, never by reaching a point total. In order: **Resonance**, **Distillation**, **Ruins**, **The Choir's Road**.
_Avoid_: tier (aspects have tiers), era, chapter

**Joining**:
One of the two ways a Listener can end their story: answering the Choir and going in after them. It is chosen per player and cannot be undone.
_Avoid_: ending (as a proper noun), ascension, good/bad ending

**Silencing**:
The other way to end the story: ending the Choir's voice for yourself. It removes temporary and lasting warp, but permanent warp stays. It is chosen per player and cannot be undone.
_Avoid_: ending (as a proper noun), banishing, good/bad ending
